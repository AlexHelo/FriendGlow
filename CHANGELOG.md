# Changelog

## v1.2.2
First public release for WoW: Forever.
- Green name and green triangle over party/raid members, friends (in-game and Battle.net), guildmates (optional) and your own list.
- Works with Blizzard nameplates and Plater.
- Settings page (Options → AddOns → FriendGlow, or `/fglow`).
- Optional: keep friendly nameplates on automatically (never changes them during combat).
- Names go back to their normal color when someone stops counting as a friend.
